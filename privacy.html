Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Text to Speech News Reader ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.

1. Personal Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII). There is no user registration, account creation, or user profile tracking required to use the App.

2. Locally Stored Data

All data generated or imported by the user is stored strictly locally on the user's mobile device using secure Android local storage (SharedPreferences and SQLite database). This includes:

  • Your subscribed RSS and podcast feed URLs.
  • Downloaded articles, read/unread flags, and bookmarks.
  • Your personal Groq API keys (used for AI features).
  • Custom reading preferences, Text-to-Speech voice settings, and custom TTS substitutions.

We have no access to this data, and it is never uploaded to our servers.

3. Third-Party Network Connections

To provide its core functions, the App makes direct outbound network connections from your device to the following third-party services:

  • RSS Feed Providers: When fetching articles, the App requests content directly from the RSS feed URLs you subscribe to.
  • Groq AI API: When utilizing translation, summarization, or chatbot features, the App sends the article text to the Groq API. This requires your personal Groq API key and is governed by Groq's privacy policy.
  • MediaPipe Local LLM: Local AI processing (such as on-device language identification or local model execution) is run entirely on your physical device and does not send any text data over the internet.

4. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the App will permanently delete all stored articles, settings, database, and API keys. You can also manually reset the database or delete individual feeds from the App's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.

8. News Content & Attribution Policy

Text to Speech News Reader functions strictly as an open RSS feed aggregator client and text-to-speech utility tool. The application does not generate, author, publish, or edit original news articles or media content. All news headlines, text summaries, and article links displayed in the app are fetched dynamically from public RSS feeds configured or subscribed to by the user.

Full copyright, ownership, and editorial responsibility belong entirely to their respective original publishers and source organizations. Direct source links and publisher attributions are provided for every article displayed within the app. If you are a news publisher and wish to request feed configuration guidance or removal, please contact the developer via email.
